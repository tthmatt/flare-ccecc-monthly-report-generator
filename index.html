Flare Dynamics

Flare Dynamics Monthly Photo Report Generator

Upload the raw photos without sorting them into site folders. The app reads each photo's GPS metadata, assigns it to the nearest CCECC site, creates one PDF per site, and packages the reports into a ZIP file. Processing happens in your browser.

Select all the original photos together. No site folders are needed.
The folder may contain subfolders; existing folder names are ignored because GPS determines the site.
Choose raw photo files or a raw photo folder to begin.

Image preview

Keep the original files: WhatsApp and other messaging apps may remove GPS metadata. Photos with missing GPS, an uncertain site match, or a location more than 750 m from every supplied site pin are flagged for review. Very large photo sets should be processed in a desktop browser.
